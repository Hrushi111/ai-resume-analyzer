💡 Quick Tips to Improve Your ATS Score

1

Use Keywords from the Job Description

Mirror the exact keywords used in the job posting. ATS systems often look for exact matches.

2

Quantify Your Achievements

Use numbers: "Increased sales by 40%" scores better than "increased sales significantly".

3

Keep Formatting Clean

Avoid tables, images, and complex formatting. Use a simple, single-column layout.

4

Include a Skills Section

List your technical and soft skills explicitly. ATS parsers specifically scan for skill keywords.
